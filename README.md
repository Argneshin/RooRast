## Features

### 1. Anonymous Messaging and Feedback Inbox
Send anonymous messages and receive honest feedback. Designed for lightweight social interaction, informal polls, and opinion sharing without revealing identity.

### 2. Automated Pre-Publication Content Review
All text submitted to anonymous messaging and anonymous chat sections is reviewed by multiple automated agents before publication for violations, abuse, threats, spam, and prohibited content. The goal is to keep the community safe and prevent misuse.

### 3. Short-Form Content and Link Sharing
Create and share short text, quotes, links, or other concise content in a minimal environment. Suitable for link introductions, short ideas, and quick references.

### 4. “How Well Do You Know Me?” Quiz
Create fun, interactive quizzes to measure how well friends and audiences know you, with shareable results.

### 5. Anonymous Chat
An environment for anonymous conversations without identity disclosure, protected by the same content-review layers used in anonymous messaging.

### 6. Lightweight Web Tools
Quick-bookmarking-style utilities, link management, and access to everyday web services that complete RooRast’s all-in-one nature.

### 7. Developer Support Page
A simple, user-friendly way to financially support or encourage the development team without complex processes.

## How It Works

1. Visit **RRast.ir** or the current address **R.RRast.ir**.
2. Choose a section: anonymous messaging, link sharing, acquaintance quiz, anonymous chat, or lightweight tools.
3. Enter your text or content.
4. In anonymous sections, content is evaluated by review agents before publication.
5. Once approved, the content is published or sent.

---

## Safety and Content Review

RooRast is built on the principle of “a more open and freer hand,” but this freedom comes with responsibility. The multi-agent review system is designed to prevent abuse, harassment, spam, threats, and illegal content. Violating content may be rejected or removed without prior notice. Users bear ultimate responsibility for the content they submit.

## Privacy and Data

RooRast may collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, visit duration, and submitted content (where necessary for review). This information is used to improve performance, maintain security, conduct content review, and respond to legal requests. Personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except where required by law. See `PRIVACY.en.md` for full details.
