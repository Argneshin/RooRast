RooRast
A multifunctional web platform for anonymous messaging, short-form content and link sharing, anonymous chat, “How well do you know me?” quizzes, and lightweight web tools — with a strict multi-layer content review system.

Features
1. Anonymous Messaging and Feedback Inbox
Send anonymous messages with mandatory multi-layer review before publication. Designed for honest feedback within a very strict rules framework.
2. Strict Automated Content Review
All text in anonymous sections is reviewed by multiple automated agents (and human review when needed) for violations including political, religious, sexual, threatening, spam, doxxing, and many other categories. Policy = Zero Tolerance.
3. Short-Form Content and Link Sharing
Create and share short text, quotes, or links in a minimal environment (fully subject to the rules).
4. “How Well Do You Know Me?” Quiz
Create interactive quizzes to measure how well friends know you, with shareable results.
5. Anonymous Chat
Anonymous conversations under the same strict review layer.
6. Lightweight Web Tools
Everyday web utilities that complete the all-in-one nature of the platform.
7. Developer Support Page
A simple way to financially support or encourage the development team.

How It Works
	1	Visit RRast.ir or the current address R.RRast.ir.
	2	Choose a section.
	3	Enter your content.
	4	In anonymous sections, content is mandatorily evaluated by the review system.
	5	Only if fully approved is the content published or sent. Otherwise it is rejected without notice.

Safety and Content Review (Strict)
RooRast is built on the principle of “freedom with very heavy responsibility.” The multi-agent review system operates under a zero-tolerance policy to prevent abuse, political content, religious content, sexual content, threats, harassment, spam, and illegal material.
	•	Violating content may be rejected or removed without prior notice.
	•	User access may be temporarily or permanently blocked.
	•	In serious cases, technical data is provided to legal authorities.
	•	Users bear full and final responsibility for submitted content.
	•	There is no guaranteed right of appeal.
Full details are in TERMS.fa.md and TERMS.en.md.

Privacy and Data
RooRast collects technical information (full IP, device, browser, time, content) for security, enforcement, and responding to legal requests. Personal data is not sold or rented except where required by law. See PRIVACY.en.md for full details.

FAQ
What is RooRast? A multifunctional platform for anonymous messaging (with strict review), link sharing, anonymous chat, acquaintance quizzes, and lightweight web tools.
Are messages truly anonymous? Anonymity is designed toward the recipient. Full technical metadata is recorded for security and enforcement and may be provided to authorities in case of violations.
Is content reviewed? Yes. Mandatory, multi-layer, zero-tolerance review.
What content is prohibited? Almost all political, religious, sexual, threatening, spam, doxxing, hate, and illegal content. Full list in TERMS.
Minimum age? 18 years old.
Is data sold? No, except where required by law.
How do I report abuse? Via @Argneshin or @RRastApp.
