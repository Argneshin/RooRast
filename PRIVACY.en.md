## 1. Introduction

This policy explains what information RooRast collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and what limited rights users have. By using the service, you agree to this policy **fully and non-negotiably**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1. Technical Information (Mandatory for Security and Enforcement)

- Full IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Screen resolution and browser language
- Pages visited and sequence
- Exact time and duration of visit
- Referrer
- Device technical identifiers (to the extent possible)
- Submitted content (message text, links, chat content) in full for review and temporary/permanent retention in case of violations

### 2.2. Optional Information

If you contact support, the Telegram/Instagram handle or any information you provide is stored.

### 2.3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies, localStorage, and similar technologies are used for session maintenance, security, performance analysis, and abuse detection. Disabling them may cause the service to malfunction, and responsibility lies with the user.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the service
- Execute the multi-layer content review system and prevent abuse
- Detect and counter violations, spam, system circumvention, and malicious activity
- Maintain technical security and prevent attacks
- Respond to lawful requests from competent authorities
- Perform aggregate, non-personal analytics to improve the product
- Cooperate with judicial and law-enforcement authorities in case of serious violations

---

## 4. Information Sharing

Personal data is **not sold, rented, or shared with commercial third parties**, except:

- Where required by law or at the formal request of competent judicial and law-enforcement authorities
- To maintain service security and prevent serious violations (to the extent necessary)
- In the event of a merger, sale, or transfer of team assets, with reasonable confidentiality safeguards

In case of detected serious violations (threats, doxxing, illegal content, etc.), related technical information may be provided to legal authorities without prior notice.

---

## 5. Security

RooRast applies customary and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However:

- No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.
- Absolute security is not guaranteed.
- Users are responsible for protecting their own devices and internet connections.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Technical and content data related to potential violations may be retained for longer periods.
- Ordinary data is retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by law.
- After that, it is deleted or anonymized, except where required by law or ongoing investigations.

---

## 7. User Rights (Limited)

Users may contact **@Argneshin** or **@RRastApp** to request access, correction, or deletion of their information.

- Requests are reviewed in accordance with legal and security requirements.
- Where information is necessary for enforcement or investigations, deletion requests may be refused.
- The team has no obligation to respond quickly or accept all requests.
