# Security Policy — RooRast

## Supported Versions
Only the current official deployment at **R.RRast.ir** and the official domain **RRast.ir** are supported for security reports.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
Please report security vulnerabilities privately to the team:

- Telegram channel: **@Argneshin**
- Official handle: **@RRastApp** (Telegram/Instagram)
- Developer: **@AsliCode**

Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before they are reviewed and addressed. Include:
- a clear description of the issue;
- steps to reproduce;
- affected URL or component;
- potential impact;
- any suggested mitigation.

## Scope
In scope:
- RRast.ir
- R.RRast.ir
- Official RooRast services and endpoints

Out of scope:
- social engineering;
- denial-of-service testing;
- spam or automated abuse;
- testing against third-party services.

## Safe Harbor
Good-faith security research that follows this policy will be reviewed cooperatively. The team reserves the right to determine whether a report qualifies for acknowledgment.
