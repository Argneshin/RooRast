# Security Policy — RooRast (Strict)

**Version 2.0 — Last Updated: 27 November 2026**

## Supported Versions

Only the current official deployments at **R.RRast.ir** and the official domain **RRast.ir** are supported for security reports. Any other mirror, fork, or unauthorized copy is out of scope and unsupported.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities **privately and responsibly** to the team:

- Telegram channel: **@Argneshin**
- Official handle: **@RRastApp** (Telegram / Instagram)
- Developer: **@AsliCode**

**Do not** publicly disclose vulnerabilities before they have been reviewed and addressed by the team.

Include in your report:

- A clear and detailed description of the issue
- Exact steps to reproduce
- Affected URL, endpoint, or component
- Potential impact and severity assessment
- Any suggested mitigation (optional)

## Scope

**In scope:**
- RRast.ir
- R.RRast.ir
- Official RooRast services, APIs, and endpoints under the control of Argneshin / AsliCode

**Out of scope:**
- Social engineering or phishing against team members or users
- Denial-of-service (DoS / DDoS) testing
- Spam or automated abuse testing
- Testing against third-party services or infrastructure not controlled by the team
- Physical security
- Issues requiring physical access or compromised user devices

## Safe Harbor

Good-faith security research that strictly follows this policy will be reviewed cooperatively. The team reserves the exclusive right to determine whether a report qualifies for acknowledgment or any form of recognition. There is no bug bounty program at this time.

Researchers who act in bad faith, publicly disclose before coordination, or cause harm may face legal action.
